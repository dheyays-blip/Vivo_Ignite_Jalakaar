Mumbai, 2026: six weeks that made the case

  1. 16 Jun

    Storage at 10.35%. BMC orders 20% cuts to industrial and commercial users.

    unverified
  2. 23 Jun

    Critical low of 8.34% — groundwater becomes emergency backup.

    unverified
  3. 25 Jun

    Monsoon rain lifts statewide storage to 53.38%.

    unverified
  4. 30 Jun

    Mumbai’s seven lakes bottom out at 6.93% — the figure the whole pitch rests on.

    verified • BMC
  5. 07 Aug

    Mumbai’s seven lakes refill to 88.50%. Pune’s five dams sit at 96.60%.

    verified • WRD
